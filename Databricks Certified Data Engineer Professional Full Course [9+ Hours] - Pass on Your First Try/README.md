## How to use these

This is the professional-level exam: 59 scored questions in 120 minutes, and the
questions are scenarios rather than recall. Work through a module video, then keep
its PDF open for review.

Section 1 is 22% on its own — more than twice any other section — so modules 02–09
deserve proportionally more of your time. The three practice exams are weighted to
the real section proportions, so sit each one under timed conditions once all 35
modules are done.
