## How to use these

Work through a module video, then keep its PDF open for review. The three practice
exams are best saved until you have finished all 32 modules — sit each one under
timed conditions before checking answers.

Module 31 covers what AWS changed after exam guide v2.1. If your other study material
is more than a few months old, read it before you trust anything that contradicts it.
