## How to use these

This is a leader-level exam: it asks which approach fits a business situation,
not how to configure anything. If you come from a hands-on background, the trap
is over-thinking — the expected answer is usually the simpler one.

Section 2 is the largest share and the fastest-moving: modules 10–18 cover
Google's actual product line, which is renamed more often than the concepts are.
Module 28 collects what has changed, and is worth reading early if your other
material is more than a few months old.

The three practice exams are best saved until you have finished all 31 modules —
sit each one under timed conditions before checking answers.
