## How to use these

Modules 01–05 are the foundations the rest of the course assumes — the development
approaches and the agile vocabulary turn up in People and Process questions alike, so
do not skip them even if predictive projects are what you work on.

Domain II is the largest share of the exam and spans modules 17–30, which crosses the
boundary between the two videos. Module 43, the numbers and graphs clinic, is worth a
second pass on its own: the calculation questions are the ones most often lost to
arithmetic rather than judgement.

The four practice exams are best saved until you have finished all 46 modules — sit
each one under timed conditions before checking answers.
