## How to use these

Sections 1 and 2 are nearly half the exam between them, and they are also where
the scenarios are longest — a question will describe a pipeline and ask which of
four plausible designs survives the constraint buried in the third sentence.
Modules 02–18 deserve more time than their share of the course suggests.

Module 40 is worth reading early. Google renames and retires data products often
enough that a fair amount of older study material is stale rather than wrong, and
knowing which is which saves re-learning things twice.

The three practice papers are best saved until you have finished all 43 modules —
sit each one under timed conditions before checking answers.
