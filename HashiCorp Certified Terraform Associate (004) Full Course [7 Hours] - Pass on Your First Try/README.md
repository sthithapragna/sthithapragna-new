## How to use these

Work through a module video, then keep its PDF open for review. The three practice
exams are best saved until you have finished all 27 modules — sit each one under
timed conditions before checking answers.

The course is taught against Terraform 1.12. Module 25 covers what has changed since,
and which differences actually alter an exam answer rather than just the CLI output.
