## How to use these

Each domain ends with a drill module of worked scenarios — modules 07, 12, 16, 22 and
25. Those are the checkpoints: if a drill goes badly, go back through that domain
before moving on rather than pressing ahead.

The three practice exams are weighted 6/4/4/4/2 across the five domains, matching the
real paper, so treat them as timed rehearsals once all 28 modules are done.
