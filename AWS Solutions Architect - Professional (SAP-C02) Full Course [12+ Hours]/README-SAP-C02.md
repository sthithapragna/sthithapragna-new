## How to use these

Work through a module video, then keep its PDF open for review. The practice exams
are organised by domain rather than as mixed papers, so you can sit one straight
after finishing that domain instead of waiting until the end.

This is a professional-level exam and the questions are long. Module 44 covers the
time budget, and module 41 the pairs that are easiest to confuse under pressure.
