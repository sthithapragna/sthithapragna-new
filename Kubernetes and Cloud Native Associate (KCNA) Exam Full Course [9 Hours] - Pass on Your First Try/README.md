## How to use these

Domain 1 is 44% of the exam on its own, so modules 01–12 are worth more of your time
than their share of the course suggests. Work through a module video, then keep its
PDF open for review.

The four practice exams are best saved until you have finished all 32 modules — sit
each one under timed conditions before checking answers.

Module 29 collects every currency flag in the course. Cloud native tooling moves fast
and a lot of older study material is stale rather than wrong, so it is worth reading
early if your other material predates this course.
