## How to use these

Module 02 sets out the 4D framework the rest of the course leans on, so watch it
before the domain modules even though it is not part of any one domain.

Domain 2, output evaluation, is the largest share at 21% — this exam cares more
about judging what Claude gave you than about writing the prompt in the first
place. Modules 06–10 are the ones to know cold.

The three practice exams are best saved until you have finished all 31 modules —
sit each one under timed conditions before checking answers.
