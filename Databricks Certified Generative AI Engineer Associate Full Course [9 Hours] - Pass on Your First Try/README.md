## How to use these

Work through a module video, then keep its PDF open for review. The three practice
exams are best saved until you have finished all 38 modules — sit each one under
timed conditions before checking answers.

Module 35 covers what changed since the published exam guide. Generative AI tooling on
Databricks moves fast enough that older study material goes stale rather than wrong,
so it is worth reading early.
