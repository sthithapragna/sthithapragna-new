## How to use these

Coming from SAP-C02, read module 50 first. It sets out what changed, and module 51
lists the services that closed, ended or were renamed — between them they are the
difference between study material that is merely dated and material that is now
wrong.

The five per-domain practice exams are fifteen questions each, so you can sit one
straight after finishing that domain rather than waiting until the end. The full
mock is a single 75-question sitting split across three parts: do all three in one
go, under time, and total the score afterwards.

Domains 1 and 2 are half the exam between them and span modules 01–24. Domains 3
and 4 are 15% each, so modules 25–39 need proportionally less time than their
length suggests.
