## How to use these

Much of this exam is reading YAML and saying what it does, so the slides are worth
more with a repository open beside them. Module 10 walks through reading a whole
workflow at exam speed and is worth a second pass on its own.

Domains 1 and 4 carry the most weight. Domain 5 is the smallest, but script
injection and OIDC (modules 28–29) turn up in questions badged as other domains
too, so do not skip them.

The three practice exams are best saved until you have finished all 35 modules —
sit each one under timed conditions before checking answers.
