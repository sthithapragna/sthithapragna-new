## How to use these

Work through a module video, then keep its PDF open for review. The three practice
exams are best saved until you have finished all 37 modules — sit each one under
timed conditions before checking answers.

Coming from AI-102, read module 33 first. It covers the answers that changed, which
matters more than usual here: a lot of older material is not wrong so much as
superseded by Foundry.
