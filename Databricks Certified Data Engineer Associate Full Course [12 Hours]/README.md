## How to use these

Work through a module video, then keep its PDF open for review. The three practice
exams are best saved until you have finished all 34 modules — sit each one under
timed conditions before checking answers.

If you studied for this exam before May 2026, read module 32 first. A lot of the
tooling was renamed, and older material is not always wrong so much as out of date.
