## How to use these

This exam tests architectural judgement rather than recall, so most questions are
a scenario with several defensible answers and one best one. Module 32 works
through the decision sequences the exam keeps returning to, and is the single most
useful module to revisit before sitting it.

Domain 3 is the largest share at 19% and spans modules 11–16. Domain 7 is the
smallest at 7%, so modules 30–31 need proportionally less of your time than their
length suggests.

The three practice papers are best saved until you have finished all 36 modules —
sit each one under timed conditions before checking answers.
